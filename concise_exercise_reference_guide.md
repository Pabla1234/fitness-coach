# CONCISE EXERCISE REFERENCE DOCUMENT

This document is designed as a **quick-reference guide for coaches and trainees**. Each exercise is explained briefly to help understand the **basic purpose, execution, and key cues**. This can be used for beginners, intermediate, and advanced athletes.

---

## 1. SQUAT (Bodyweight / Dumbbell / Barbell)
**Muscles Worked:** Quadriceps, Glutes, Hamstrings, Core  
**Purpose:** Builds lower-body strength and functional movement.

**Execution Basics:**
- Stand with feet shoulder-width apart
- Push hips back and bend knees
- Lower until thighs are parallel to the ground
- Push through heels to stand back up

**Key Cues:** Chest up, spine neutral, knees aligned with toes

---

## 2. PUSH-UP
**Muscles Worked:** Chest, Shoulders, Triceps, Core  
**Purpose:** Upper-body strength and core stability.

**Execution Basics:**
- Hands slightly wider than shoulders
- Body in straight line from head to heels
- Lower chest toward the floor
- Push back up to starting position

**Key Cues:** Tight core, controlled movement

---

## 3. BENCH PRESS (Barbell / Dumbbell)
**Muscles Worked:** Chest, Triceps, Front Deltoids  
**Purpose:** Primary chest mass and strength builder.

**Execution Basics:**
- Lie flat on bench with feet planted
- Lower weight to mid-chest
- Press upward until arms are extended

**Key Cues:** Controlled descent, elbows not flared

---

## 4. DEADLIFT
**Muscles Worked:** Lower Back, Glutes, Hamstrings, Core  
**Purpose:** Total-body strength and posterior chain development.

**Execution Basics:**
- Bar close to shins
- Hinge at hips with flat back
- Drive hips forward to stand

**Key Cues:** Lift with legs and hips, not lower back

---

## 5. PULL-UP / LAT PULLDOWN
**Muscles Worked:** Lats, Upper Back, Biceps  
**Purpose:** Back width and upper-body pulling strength.

**Execution Basics:**
- Grip bar wider than shoulders
- Pull chest toward bar
- Lower slowly with control

**Key Cues:** Squeeze shoulder blades, avoid swinging

---

## 6. SHOULDER PRESS
**Muscles Worked:** Shoulders, Triceps, Upper Chest  
**Purpose:** Shoulder strength and overhead stability.

**Execution Basics:**
- Start with weights at shoulder level
- Press upward until arms are extended
- Lower back under control

**Key Cues:** Core tight, avoid lower-back arch

---

## 7. BICEP CURL
**Muscles Worked:** Biceps, Forearms  
**Purpose:** Arm size and elbow flexion strength.

**Execution Basics:**
- Elbows close to torso
- Curl weight upward
- Lower slowly

**Key Cues:** No momentum, full range of motion

---

## 8. TRICEP DIPS / PUSHDOWNS
**Muscles Worked:** Triceps  
**Purpose:** Arm strength and definition.

**Execution Basics:**
- Keep elbows tucked
- Extend arms fully
- Control the return

**Key Cues:** Move only forearms

---

## 9. JUMPING JACKS
**Muscles Worked:** Full Body, Cardiovascular System  
**Purpose:** Warm-up and calorie burn.

**Execution Basics:**
- Jump feet out while raising arms
- Return to starting position

**Key Cues:** Light on feet, steady pace

---

## 10. BURPEES
**Muscles Worked:** Full Body  
**Purpose:** High-intensity fat loss and conditioning.

**Execution Basics:**
- Squat down, kick feet back
- Perform push-up
- Jump upward

**Key Cues:** Maintain rhythm, land softly

---

## 11. MOUNTAIN CLIMBERS
**Muscles Worked:** Core, Shoulders, Legs  
**Purpose:** Core endurance and cardio.

**Execution Basics:**
- Start in plank position
- Drive knees alternately toward chest

**Key Cues:** Hips low, fast controlled movement

---

## 12. DYNAMIC WARM-UP
**Purpose:** Injury prevention and performance readiness.

**Includes:**
- Arm circles
- Leg swings
- Hip rotations

**Key Cues:** Smooth and controlled movements

---

## 13. COOLDOWN & STRETCHING
**Purpose:** Recovery, flexibility, soreness reduction.

**Execution Basics:**
- Stretch major muscle groups
- Hold each stretch 20–30 seconds
- Breathe deeply

**Key Cues:** No bouncing, gentle stretch

---

## USAGE NOTES
- Beginners: Focus on form and light resistance
- Intermediate: Increase volume and intensity
- Advanced: Progressive overload and tempo control
- Weight Loss: Short rest, higher intensity
- Weight Gain: Heavier loads, longer rest
